Support

Need help?

Write to plainlauncher.app@icloud.com. Include your iPhone model and iOS version; replies usually within two days.

Common questions

How do I add the widget?
Hold the Home Screen → Edit → Add Widget → Plain Launcher → choose a size.
How do I choose the apps?
Hold the widget → Edit Widget → Row 1 → Choose → Open App… → pick the app. Any action from the list works too.
How do I remove a row?
In Edit Widget, lower “Rows shown”, or re-pick the rows below the one you want gone.
The widget settings say “Unable to Load”.
Close the sheet and open it again. This is an iOS behaviour; your rows are kept.
I deleted an app and its row is still there.
iOS keeps the row; re-pick it or lower “Rows shown”.
Can it block apps or limit screen time?
No. Plain Launcher only opens things.
Why isn’t the widget pure black?
iOS draws a glass edge on every widget. Try the Clear icon style — the widget then looks like the rest of the screen.
How do I hide app names and the Search button?
Hold the Home Screen → Edit → Customize → Large. Search: Settings → Home Screen & App Library.
