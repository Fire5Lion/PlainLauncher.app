Need help?

Write to plainlauncher.app@icloud.com. Include your iPhone model and iOS version; replies usually within two days.

Common questions

Add the widget
Hold the Home Screen → Edit → Add Widget → Plain Launcher → Pick a size → Add Widget
Choose apps
Hold the widget → Edit Widget → Row 1 → Choose → Open App… → Pick the app
Does it block apps?
No. Plain Launcher only shows text rows that open apps. Nothing is blocked, timed or tracked. The goal is a calmer Home Screen, not restrictions; for limits, use Screen Time in iOS.
How do I add actions?
Hold the widget, tap Edit Widget, then tap Choose next to a row. Pick any action, such as New Note, Timer or Find My, instead of Open App….
How do I remove a row?
Lower Rows shown in Edit Widget: the widget shows only the first rows. To remove a row from the middle, choose the apps below it again, one row higher, then lower Rows shown.
Can it show the weather?
No. Weather needs network and location access, and Plain Launcher uses neither.
Can I tap the time?
Yes: in Edit Widget, set Time tap to Open App… and pick Clock (Date tap works the same way, for example with Calendar).
I deleted an app, but its row stays.
iOS keeps the row with the old name, and tapping it does nothing. Hold the widget, tap Edit Widget, and choose another app for that row or, if it's the last row, lower Rows shown.
Edit Widget says "Unable to Load"
This message comes from iOS. Close the sheet and open Edit Widget again.
Why isn't the widget pure black?
iOS lifts very dark widget backgrounds and draws a glass edge around the widget. Pick a dark wallpaper so it blends in.
Clear or Default icons?
Theme colors apply only with the Default icon style. With Clear or Tinted, iOS draws the widget (glass) itself. Clear plus a wallpaper from the Wallpaper tab looks the most native.
How do I hide app names?
Hold the Home Screen, tap Edit → Customize, then choose Large.
How do I hide the Search button?
Open Settings → Home Screen & App Library and turn off the Search button.
Can I still find all my apps?
Yes. All your apps stay in the App Library: swipe left past the last Home Screen page. You can search there too.
Is there a Lock Screen widget?
Yes. Plain Launcher also offers a Lock Screen widget with up to 3 rows. Customize your Lock Screen and add the widget there.
What about privacy?
Nothing leaves your phone: no account, no network, no analytics. Your theme is stored on the device.
