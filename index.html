Plain Launcher
Text widgets for a quieter Home Screen

Your apps, as plain text. Tap a line, it opens.

Home Screen widgets that show the apps and actions you choose as text rows. No icons, no quiz, no subscription. One-time purchase.

Download on the App Store

Requires iOS 27 or later.

How it works

Add the widget, hold it, choose the apps — or actions like “New Note” or “Find My”. Rows open apps instantly through iOS; nothing goes through a shortcut or a redirect.

What it doesn’t do

It doesn’t block apps, track your time or collect anything. It’s a launcher, not a parent.

Make it yours

Six presets, your own fonts, sizes, colors and row markers. A wallpaper generator that matches the widget. Optional time and date in the header.
