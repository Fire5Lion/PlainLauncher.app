Plain Launcher
Privacy Policy

Nothing leaves your phone.

Last updated: October 2026

Plain Launcher does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no network connection.

What the app stores

Your theme settings are saved on your device in a container shared between the app and its widget. Which apps your widget rows open is stored by iOS as part of the widget configuration; the app never sees it.

Photos

If you save a generated wallpaper, the app asks for permission to add images to your photo library. It never reads your photos.

Third parties

None. No SDKs, no trackers, no crash reporting.
